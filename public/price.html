Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to start the free tier?

No, you do not need a credit card to use the Starter tier. Simply click "Get started free", open WhatsApp, and begin chatting immediately.

How does the 7-day trial work?

When you select the Growth plan, you will get full access to all premium features for 7 days. You can cancel at any time within those 7 days and you won't be charged.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Absolutely. You can manage your billing preferences directly inside the WhatsApp chat menu or through your billing portal link, allowing you to upgrade, downgrade, or pause at any time.

What counts as an "AI chat query"?

A query is counted every time you ask a question or request a recommendation and the AI assistant responds. General account management commands do not count toward your limit.
